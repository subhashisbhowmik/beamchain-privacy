Beam Chain: Privacy Policy

Snell & Hooke · Last updated 31 October 2026

Beam Chain is a puzzle game for Android made by Snell & Hooke. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing about who you are. Beam Chain has no accounts or sign-in, never asks for your name, email or contacts, does not use your location, and does not use the Android advertising ID. The current version shows no advertising.

Optional anonymous play statistics

The first time you open the game it asks whether you want to share anonymous play statistics. Nothing is sent unless you tap Share, and you can turn it off at any time with the chart button on the home screen. If you agree, the game sends:

  • Game activity: which screens you open; for each level you play, when you start it, whether you solved it or left, the stars earned, the time spent, and how many pieces you placed, turned, moved or removed, how many times you pressed Run or Reset, and how many hints you used.
  • Crash reports: when the game crashes or hits an error, a technical report of what went wrong (the error and where in the game's code it happened).
  • Device and app information collected automatically by the tools below: device model, Android version, screen size, language, app version, country (worked out from the network address, which is not stored), and a random identifier for this installation of the game.

We use this only to find levels that are too hard or too easy, to see where players stop playing, and to fix crashes. It is not used for advertising, not combined with other data about you, and not sold.

The statistics are processed for us by Google, using Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, under Google's terms (see Firebase privacy and security). Data is encrypted in transit. Statistics are kept for 2 months and crash reports for 90 days, then deleted.

Because the statistics are tied only to a random installation identifier and not to you, we cannot pick out a particular person's data. Turning statistics off stops all collection from then on; uninstalling the game or clearing its data also discards the identifier.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, unlocked tools, achievements) and settings (sound, music, vibration, statistics choice) are saved only on your device, so you can carry on where you left off. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game, and the internet, which it uses only to send the optional statistics above and to check Google Play for updates.

Children

Beam Chain is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If a future version handles data in other ways, such as advertising or optional in-app purchases, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play data safety section before that version is released.
