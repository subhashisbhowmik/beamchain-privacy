Beam Chain: Privacy Policy

Snell & Hooke · Last updated 31 October 2026

Beam Chain is a puzzle game for Android made by Snell & Hooke. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing. Beam Chain does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts or sign-in, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and the current version shows no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, unlocked tools, achievements) and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your device, so you can carry on where you left off. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game. Its Android package also lists the standard internet permission, which the game does not use to send any data.

Children

Beam Chain is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data, such as advertising or optional in-app purchases, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play data safety section before that version is released.
